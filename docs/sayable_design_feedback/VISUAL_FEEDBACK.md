# Sayable visual feedback — 8 Nov 2026

These screenshots were supplied by the project owner. Claude Code should inspect **all nine** as ground-truth examples of current design failure before redesigning. The filenames are descriptive, not code mappings. Find corresponding sections in the actual running website before editing.

## Non-negotiable direction
- Primary references: Apple product pages (hero scale and product-led storytelling); Miligram (holistic art direction, expressive pacing); RTM Audio (single product traveling through explanatory scroll chapters).
- Secondary references: Mobius (motion and spatial continuity), Incroy (payoff / final footer transition), Davide Carusio (strong typography and restrained aesthetic), Premotio (controlled distortions and reveals), transitions.dev (microinteraction recipes).
- No generic 50/50 split layout, identical alternating rows, click-to-understand feature tabs, decorative orange monospaced step numbers, gimmicky highlight color, or massive text walls.
- Preserve the working software and keep public/legal claims accurate. No fabricated customer results, fake PDF/UA certification, invented ROI, or misleading accessibility guarantees.

## Screenshots and critique
- `01_hero_blue_emphasis.png`: Hero: oversized generic dark headline; blue screen-reader emphasis; overlong copy; visually empty space before document, unclear city outcome.
- `02_demo_toggled.png`: Document demo: split as-posted / after-repair toggle burdens visitor; convert to auto-progressing accessible scroll chapter with optional user controls.
- `03_orange_step_numbers.png`: Orange mono 01-05 counters have no semantic value; remove or replace with meaningful milestones and images.
- `04_inbox_mono.png`: Mono Inbox label is generic/decorative and disconnected from style; use real UI typography only where it signals file identifiers.
- `05_five_stage_tabs.png`: Five equal steps, giant header, tabs and white space: make coherent scroll-controlled document workflow; show actual availability for each stage.
- `06_competitor_copy_wall.png`: Acrobat/ChatGPT comparison: enormous text wall and table; distill to one sharp differentiation and real evidence; do not disparage competitor.
- `07_cost_estimator_density.png`: Sliders, ranges, labels too crowded; progressive disclosure, illustrative scenario, clear math with no fabricated savings.
- `08_sample_report_density.png`: Report: huge white space to right and simulated verification, tiny metadata; make hierarchy, focus, proof and disclaimers better.
- `09_how_it_works_repeated_rows.png`: How-it-works: repetitive rows 1-5; show three distinct buyer steps with specific deliverables and visual evidence.

## Intended experience
1. Within five seconds: what Sayable does, for whom, and the next step.
2. One visually convincing PDF example illustrates the assistive-tech accessibility gap (no automatic audio).
3. Natural-scroll sequence evolves one document into an inventory, triage, controlled repair, verification and a reviewable report. Present roadmap stages honestly.
4. Clear government deadline (DOJ revised deadlines: Apr 26 2027 for >=50k and Apr 26 2028 for <50k/special districts; verify with official sources; exceptions and effective-communication duties apply).
5. Honest business differentiation, cost planning with explicit assumptions, actual deliverable, clear audit CTA.
6. Supporting product/how-it-works/solutions pages can be navigated with meaningful, fast, reduced-motion-aware transitions, no jarring blank flash or jump.
7. Test screenshots at 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 390, 360, including every scroll chapter.
