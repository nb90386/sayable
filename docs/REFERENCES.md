## Principles carried into DESIGN.md
1. One mechanism, everywhere: the reading path appears in the wordmark, the hero, the ribbons, the lifecycle, the report and the close.
2. The document is the product shot. Show the file, not a dashboard.
3. Value in plain arithmetic, with assumptions written next to the numbers.
4. Warm, but institutional: a light porcelain ground, evergreen authority, apricot only where something snags.
5. No borrowed proof: no logos, no testimonials, no counters.
