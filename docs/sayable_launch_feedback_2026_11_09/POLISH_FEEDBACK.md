## Core creative direction

- Preserve ~90% of the existing refined Sayable visual identity; prioritize intelligent refinement, not a wholesale rebrand.
- Homepage: show the verified Title II deadlines, with a real, accessible live countdown to April 26, 2027 (larger covered governments), optional alternative April 26, 2028 for smaller entities/special districts. Cite current DOJ guidance; describe total population correctly, clarify exceptions and existing communication obligations.
- Follow with a respectful, accurately labeled fictional first-person screen-reader interaction with a municipal permit/benefits form; show unlabeled fields/poor reading order then the scan, review, and clearer corrected document. User-initiated audio with transcript, never an unvalidated claim of actual screen-reader capture.
- Connect scanner -> city inventory -> prioritization -> repair/review -> evidence -> Readiness Audit, with reversible native-scroll-driven motion, no autoplay scan.
- Distinguish a real May 21, 2026 NFB lawsuit challenging the 2026 deadline EXTENSION from direct suits against cities. A filed challenge does not automatically shorten the legally effective dates. Verify docket before describing its status.
- Do NOT place competitor brand names/logos in sales copy. Compare workflows generically and accurately; research competitors privately.
- Contact form and real lead delivery must be VERIFIED before saying outreach-ready; if no inbox/recipient is configured, mark blocking issue, do not invent an address.
