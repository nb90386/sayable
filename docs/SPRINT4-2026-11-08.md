## The scan

- **No arrival animation.**
  - The scan position is `scanFrom(scrollProgress)` and nothing else: no timer, no keyframes, no loop.
  - At rest the line sits at the top edge of the form, and the first problem (the title not recognized as a heading) is already marked. The sighted visitor sees a problem before scrolling.
- **One source drives everything.** The line, the margin marks, the scan log and the four corrections all derive from the same scroll value, so scrolling back up reverses them exactly. A reload restores the state from the scroll position.
- **Find, explain, correct, verify.** When the line reaches an element, a dashed vermilion box and its plain-language finding appear. A little further on, the illustrative correction replaces it:
  - a solid viridian box with the structure's new name ("Heading", "Applicant name", "Type of work", grouped)
  - the ordered reading path, replacing the tangled one
  - in the log, the problem struck through and the correction stated
  - the numbered mark turning viridian
- **Specialists.** The technical identifiers stay under "Technical details".
- **Phones.** The scan waits until the form has settled on screen, and the log shows only the correction once an item is fixed.
- **Continuity.** After the scan, the same form shrinks into the city collection (unchanged from the previous pass).

## Blockers and owner decisions

- **Contact delivery.** `/api/contact` returns 503, "not configured", because `CONTACT_WEBHOOK_URL` and `CONTACT_WEBHOOK_SECRET` are not set in Vercel. The owner must choose a destination. No confirmation email was built, because nothing sends mail yet.
- **Claims to confirm.** The Agenda Desk "24 to 48 hour" turnaround and the veraPDF claims on `/verify`.
- **Testing not done.** Real-device and screen-reader testing has not been done.
- **Not adopted.** The study suggested removing the header dropdowns. They were kept: they work, they are tested, and taking them out is a product decision.
