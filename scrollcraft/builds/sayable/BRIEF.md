# Sayable: Scroll Craft brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The founder wrote: "Do not unnecessarily ask me to choose fonts or colors. Use your best creative judgment." Topics answered from the founder's Nov 5 2026 brief are marked (brief); my own decisions are marked (authored).

1. **Vibe and references.**
   - Vibe (authored from brief): human, exact, warm, institutional, quietly bold.
   - References outside the web (authored): a proofreader's red-pen markup on a galley; a theatre's printed programme beside its prompt book; an audio describer's script.
2. **Journey** (brief, section 8):
   - Exclusion: a document looks readable but works poorly; hear it.
   - Scale: one document becomes a whole inventory.
   - Clarity: the inventory is sorted.
   - Improvement: one document moves through inspection, repair, validation and review.
   - Value: a transparent estimator.
   - Trust: traceable findings and human review.
   - Decision: back to the document, now understandable. Request a Readiness Audit.
3. **Energy** (authored): calm open, then the loudest moment at the audio, then busy (inventory), then precise and calm (journey, estimator), then steady (trust), then a quiet, resolved close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment** (brief plus authored): see the feeling curve below. The moment is hearing the same form read twice.
5. **What no other site does** (authored from brief): the page itself has two stories, and the line between them is the document's reading path.
6. **Range** (authored): editorial and premium-minimal with one loud colour; not maximalist.
7. **World** (authored): distinct scenes on one split, not a continuous camera world.
8. **Assets** (brief): none supplied. The world is illustrated and typographic: documents drawn in SVG, audio from text-to-speech, clearly labelled. No photography or generated imagery.

## Grammar: Split stage (2.7)
The comparison is the product: the visible story against the accessible story. Why the other seven lost:
- **Filmic one-shot:** no footage, and it is the default the skill warns against.
- **Chaptered editorial:** fits the substance, but it hides the comparison, which is the whole point.
- **Live surface:** the product has no shippable UI yet, and the honesty rule bars painting one.
- **Continuous world:** the brief says do not build one camera flight.
- **Typographic poster:** too thin for the estimator and trust content.
- **Gallery:** nothing to catalogue.
- **Rhythmic cutlist:** too loud for public-sector buyers.

Bans honoured: no `pan`, no spotlight, no magnet, at most one scrub (none used), no drift. Two grounds that hold: porcelain on the left, graphite on the right.

## Signature move: the divider is the reading path
A fixed vertical line between the two stories carries the labels for both sides and the current chapter. Its geometry is the document's reading order:
- At the top it kinks and doubles back, with vermilion knots where the order breaks.
- Each chapter straightens it a little.
- At the close it runs straight, then travels to the left edge, and the accessible column takes the full width: the collapse.

On phones the divider becomes a sticky top rule with the same label and the same straightening line drawn horizontally.

## Feeling curve
| Act | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Title | Intrigue | Two headlines, one page: "A document can look right." beside "And still leave someone out." |
| I Exclusion | Discomfort, then recognition (**peak**) | Pressing "Hear the problem" and "Hear the difference" on the same visible form |
| II Scale | Weight | A wall of finished-looking files on the left, their real state on the right |
| III Journey | Relief through precision | The left page never changes while the right structure is repaired stage by stage |
| IV Value | Control | The visitor's own numbers become the arithmetic |
| V Trust | Steadiness | Software checks beside human checks, with a finding you can open |
| VI Decision | Resolution | The divider collapses and one clear page asks for one action |

**The peak:** "It's the site where you hear the same permit form read twice, and the first time you can't tell what any of the boxes are." It lives in Act I and gets the most scroll room.

**Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where the page is split in half and the middle line slowly untangles as you read."

## Score
| Act | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Title | parallax (sheet planes, pointer) | Depth without motion on load |
| I | pointer / audio interaction | The visitor acts, then listens |
| II | reveal | Untagged becomes tagged, a change of state |
| III | pin + structure stages | The left holds while the right advances |
| IV | count | Real numbers from the visitor's inputs |
| V | flow + in | Calm, readable substance |
| VI | collapse (signature) | The ending |

Five device families, none repeated in adjacent acts.

**Authored silence:** the gap between the title and Act I is a short, intentional breath, not dead scroll.
