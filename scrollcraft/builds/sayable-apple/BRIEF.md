# Sayable, "the Apple standard": Scroll Craft brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Answers are taken from the founder's Nov 5 2026 brief.

1. **Vibe** (brief): premium, product-first, calm, human, precise. References outside the web (authored): a museum object photographed on seamless paper; a conservator's raking-light inspection of a manuscript.
2. **Journey** (brief, section 7): opening, then the human problem (with audio), scale, product, business case, trust, what the customer gets, conclusion.
3. **Energy** (authored): quiet open; a rising reveal; the loudest moment at the audio; precise and steady through product, estimator and trust; a quiet, resolved close.
4. **Feeling and the moment:** see the curve below. The moment: the scan line passes over a perfectly normal permit form, and the form's broken insides show through.
5. **What no other site does** (authored): the document is X-rayed by your scroll.
6. **Range** (brief): premium-minimal, Apple-dominant.
7. **World** (authored): distinct scenes, carried by one ground that shifts between light and dark. Not a single continuous camera flight.
8. **Assets:** none supplied. Documents and interface are drawn in SVG and HTML; the audio is labelled text-to-speech.

## Grammar: Filmic one-shot (2.1)
The founder asked for an Apple-style product presentation: one linear argument in which the visitor is carried. Why the others lost:
- **Split stage:** the founder rejected it explicitly.
- **Continuous world:** needs a worldflight camera and footage we do not have.
- **Chaptered editorial:** forbids a centred hero and pinned scenes.
- **Live surface:** would make the whole page a tool.
- **Typographic poster:** too thin for the estimator.
- **Gallery and rhythmic cutlist:** nothing to catalogue, and too loud for public-sector buyers.

Bans honoured: no visible sequence (no chapter numbers or progress readout), no hard cuts between grounds (the ground drifts between light and dark), one entry point.

## Signature move: the structure scan
In the opening scene the permit document sits large in a pinned stage. Scrolling moves a thin blue scan line down the page. Above the line the paper is as residents see it. Below it, the hidden structure shows through: missing names, a heading tagged as text, a footer read first. The same document returns resolved at the close.

## Feeling curve
| Act | Feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Curiosity | A beautiful, ordinary form, large and calm |
| Reveal (inside the opening) | Unease | The scan shows the broken structure under the paper |
| Problem | Recognition (**peak**) | Hearing the same form read twice |
| Scale | Weight, then relief | Forty files settle into five lanes |
| Product | Understanding | One workspace, five stages, the same file |
| Business case | Control | The visitor's own numbers |
| Trust | Steadiness | What software checks, what people check, a finding you can open |
| Offer | Clarity | Exactly what an audit delivers |
| Close | Resolution | The same document, now readable in order |

**Peak sentence:** "You hear the permit form read twice, and the first time you cannot tell what any box is for."
